 WARRIOR FIELD GUIDE

Weapons, shields and magic

The game pauses while this manual is open. Complete AWS tasks in your account, then defeat a monster to grade your work. Magic kills use the same grading rules as melee kills.

Controls

WASD: move and turn. Shift: run. Space: melee attack. X or Cast: cast your selected spell. Choose I, II or III for its power. R: hold to block. Q: dodge. Scroll: zoom. Shift + scroll: cycle weapons. Click weapon or shield icons to equip; 1 / 2 / 0 select Sword / Axe / Unarmed. E near a guide: get a task. T near a gate: travel. F near a chest: open it.

All weapons

Unarmed uses your full Power because no weapon is equipped; weapon multipliers apply only to equipped items.

All magic

Every slash-separated value is I / II / III. Damage scales with your current Power. Level-I damage multipliers are Lightning 6.4x, Fireball 5.2x, Frost wave 3.2x, Thunderstorm 4.4x and Shockwave 3.6x, compared with at most 3x for melee weapons. Levels II / III multiply spell damage by 2 / 3; damage is rounded to a whole number. Listed damage is per enemy hit, except Lightning's chain bounces deal 50%, then 25% of its first hit. Higher powers add 0 / 4 / 8 range.

All spells cost 1 / 2 / 3 MP. Cooldown is shared across spells, so switching cannot bypass it. All spells are blocked by line-of-sight obstacles; face your target for Lightning, Fireball and Frost wave. A missed cast still spends MP.

MP comes only from passed voice activities: 60-74 earns 5 MP, 75-89 earns 10 MP, and 90-100 earns 15 MP, credited up to the 100 MP capacity. Each activity rewards once. Reading, repeated passes, kills, waiting and missions never replenish MP. Dialogs, loading, travel and death block casting.

Super monsters

Look for the orange SUPER MONSTER nameplate: Frostpeak Titan, Sunspire Overlord or Vault Nightlord. They are larger, tougher and hit harder. They respawn normally and give no extra reading badges or mission marks.
