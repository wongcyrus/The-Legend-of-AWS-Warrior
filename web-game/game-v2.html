 WARRIOR FIELD GUIDE

Weapons, shields and magic

The game pauses while this manual is open. Complete AWS tasks in your account, then defeat a monster to grade your work. Magic kills use the same grading rules as melee kills.

How to play & quest loop

1. Set your API key using Set Key in the top bar (or explore freely without a key). 2. Approach any guide with a golden marker (Cloudhaven Keeper, Highland Knight, Sunspire Guardian, or Vault Construct) and press E to receive an AWS task. 3. Build the requested AWS resources in your AWS account, then defeat any monster to trigger automated grading. Each passed mission restores a village building and unlocks cosmetic weapon finishes.

Controls

WASD: move and turn. Shift: run. Space: melee attack. X or Cast: unleash your selected cloud art. 3-7: toggle cloud arts. Each art has a fixed MP cost; there are no power levels. R: hold to block (press just before impact for a Perfect Parry). Q: dodge. Scroll: zoom. Shift + scroll or C / V: cycle weapons and shields. Click weapon or shield icons to equip; 1 / 2 / 0 select Sword / Axe / Unarmed. E near a guide: get a task. Walk into a glowing gate at a road end to travel automatically. F near a chest: open it. You can also click the interactive action chips in the bottom control bar.

All magic

Select several arts to cast them together with X: MP costs are added and the longest shared cooldown applies. The entire selection is reserved in one server transaction. Insufficient MP casts nothing. Selecting all five automatically changes the cast button to Global Deployment (全域部署): 87 MP with a 60-second shared cooldown. Casting all five has a 50% chance to unleash Global Deployment (全域部署 — four 6x Cloud Avatars and sky judgement pillars) and a 50% chance to awaken The Rumbling · Titan Stampede (地鳴・全域踐踏 — the Founding Titan's colossal skeletal ribcage at the origin and a wall of 7.5x crimson Colossal Titans marching outward in scalding steam). Their three waves expand across a 128-unit radius around the casting position over 1.2 seconds each and defeat enemies, including bosses, when they reach them; buildings and solid walls block hits. Wave visuals and damage use the same expanding radius. The camera pulls back to frame the attack and smoothly returns to your chosen zoom afterwards; nearby walls can limit the view. The avatars and titans remain for six seconds and do not add collision or invulnerability. Deselect any art to return to a normal combination without these execution waves. Travel, death and account changes remove the formation and cancel pending waves. On reload, your saved primary art is selected.

Damage scales with Power. Storage's listed damage is the total of six pulses plus its final burst. Army summons five shadows of your character, advancing forward and attacking three times each; listed damage is each shadow's total across three hits. Walls stop shadows and each strike checks nearby enemies. Event lightning chains to three targets at 100%, 50%, then 25% damage. The ward deals damage once per intruder and halves incoming damage while you remain inside. Recovery has no attack damage; its anchor must finish a one-second windup and have a clear landing spot.

Cooldown is shared across arts, so switching cannot bypass it. Obstacles block attacks. Fields stay where cast; moving away does not move them. Travel, account changes and death remove fields, clones and recovery anchors. These are fictional AWS-inspired combat metaphors, not actual AWS service capabilities.

Shields & Perfect Parry

World regions, dynamic events & bestiary

Explore four interconnected regions: Cloudlands Village, Frostpeak Highlands (Tailwind: +12% movement speed in northern highlands), Sunspire Desert (Sunspire Oasis: +2 HP/s regeneration), and the nine-room Forgotten Vault (Compute Overclock: +15% melee attack speed in the central chamber). Periodic AZ Outage Alerts mark an amber zone on your minimap—defeat 2 monsters near the outage within 45 seconds to trigger a High Availability Surge (+20% movement and attack speed for 20s and +20 HP). Across the four maps you will encounter 16 distinct creature types including Whispering Treants, Mirrorwater Crabs, Cloud Hornets, Crimson Demons, Sunspire Wyverns, Void Stalkers, Vault Sentinels, Highland Stags, and Bamboo Guardians.

Super monsters

Look for the orange SUPER MONSTER nameplate: Frostpeak Titan (Glacial Shockwave & chill), Sunspire Overlord (3-way Spike Volley), or Vault Nightlord (Shadow Step ambush). They are larger, tougher and hit harder. They respawn normally and give no extra reading badges or mission marks.
