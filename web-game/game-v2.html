 WARRIOR FIELD GUIDE

Weapons, shields and magic

The game pauses while this manual is open. Complete AWS tasks in your account, then defeat a monster to grade your work. Magic kills use the same grading rules as melee kills.

Controls

WASD: move and turn. Shift: run. Space: melee attack. X or Cast: unleash your selected cloud art. 3-7: toggle cloud arts. Each art has a fixed MP cost; there are no power levels. R: hold to block (press just before impact for a Perfect Parry). Q: dodge. Scroll: zoom. Shift + scroll or C / V: cycle weapons and shields. Click weapon or shield icons to equip; 1 / 2 / 0 select Sword / Axe / Unarmed. E near a guide: get a task. Walk into a glowing gate at a road end to travel automatically. F near a chest: open it.

All weapons

Unarmed uses your full Power because no weapon is equipped; weapon multipliers apply only to equipped items.

All magic

Select several arts to cast them together with X: MP costs are added and the longest shared cooldown applies. The entire selection is reserved in one server transaction. Insufficient MP casts nothing. Selecting all five automatically changes the cast button to Global Deployment (全域部署): 87 MP with a 60-second shared cooldown. Its announcement reads both the Chinese and English names. Four Cloud Avatars, each six times your size, appear ahead, behind, left and right, facing outward. Their three slashes release waves across a 128-unit radius around the casting position, marked by an outer boundary ring. Each wave expands to that boundary over 1.2 seconds and defeats enemies, including bosses, when it reaches them; buildings and solid walls block hits. Wave visuals and damage use the same expanding radius. The camera pulls back to frame the attack and smoothly returns to your chosen zoom afterwards; nearby walls can limit the view. The avatars remain for six seconds and do not add collision or invulnerability. Deselect any art to return to a normal combination without these execution waves. Travel, death and account changes remove the avatars and cancel pending waves. On reload, your saved primary art is selected.

Damage scales with Power. Storage's listed damage is the total of six pulses plus its final burst. Army summons five shadows of your character, advancing forward and attacking three times each; listed damage is each shadow's total across three hits. Walls stop shadows and each strike checks nearby enemies. Event lightning chains to three targets at 100%, 50%, then 25% damage. The ward deals damage once per intruder and halves incoming damage while you remain inside. Recovery has no attack damage; its anchor must finish a one-second windup and have a clear landing spot.

Cooldown is shared across arts, so switching cannot bypass it. Obstacles block attacks. Fields stay where cast; moving away does not move them. Travel, account changes and death remove fields, clones and recovery anchors. These are fictional AWS-inspired combat metaphors, not actual AWS service capabilities.

MP comes only from passed voice activities: 60-74 earns 5 MP, 75-89 earns 10 MP, and 90-100 earns 15 MP, credited up to the 100 MP capacity. Each activity rewards once. Reading, repeated passes, kills, waiting and missions never replenish MP. Dialogs, loading, travel and death block casting.

Super monsters

Look for the orange SUPER MONSTER nameplate: Frostpeak Titan, Sunspire Overlord or Vault Nightlord. They are larger, tougher and hit harder. They respawn normally and give no extra reading badges or mission marks.
